This will be the first stage of many, focusing on cellular life before the next stage of "animal" tier life.
The evolution will flow like that of the game "Spore", but will add fantasy elements to evolution, such as being able to grow into different branches of life, such as sentient plants, fungi, or animals.

## Design Principles

Ensure the design follows these principles:
- **Early Game (0-5 minutes):** Manual clicking → first automation → basic resource loop
- **Mid Game (5-15 minutes):** Multiple organelles → storage increases → waste management becomes relevant
- **Late Game (15-30 minutes):** Evolution path choice → DNA generation → pushing toward Cellular Differentiation
- **Balance:** Resources should gate progression naturally without excessive grinding
- **Depth:** Multiple viable strategies and building purchase orders
- **Polish:** Clear tooltips, unlock conditions, and progression milestones
